# Privacy

This page covers the convt.app website as it is today: a single page announcing that convt is coming soon.

## What this site collects

Nothing on purpose. The site has no accounts, forms, newsletter sign-up or checkout. It sets no cookies, runs no analytics or tracking scripts, and loads nothing from other websites. Its fonts and images are served from convt.app itself.

## Hosting

The site is hosted on Vercel. Like any web host, Vercel receives the technical details of each request, such as your IP address, browser user agent and the page you asked for, and keeps them in short-lived logs to run and protect the service. convt does not use those logs to track or profile visitors. See the [Vercel privacy policy](https://vercel.com/legal/privacy-policy) for how Vercel handles them.

## The convt app

The convt app converts files on your own computer and does not upload them. Optional cloud conversions, accounts and payments do not exist yet. Before any of them launch, this page will be replaced with a full privacy policy that explains what they collect and why.

## Changes

If anything above changes, this page will be updated first.
