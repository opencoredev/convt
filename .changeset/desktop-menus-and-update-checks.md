On macOS, the menu bar has convt, File, Edit, Window and Help menus, with About convt, Check for Updates…, Settings… (⌘,) and the usual shortcuts. convt checks for updates at every launch and every 5 hours while automatic checks are on, and the Updates card in Settings shows this version, when it last checked, a Check now button and any newer build with its release notes and Download.
