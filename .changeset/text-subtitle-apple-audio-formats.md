Nine new formats. Markdown converts to HTML and plain text, and HTML, Word and other documents convert to Markdown. CSV, TSV, JSON and YAML convert between each other, and CSV and TSV become HTML, Markdown or text tables, all without the document pack. SRT and WebVTT subtitles convert to each other and to plain text, and MP4, MOV, MKV and WebM videos give up their subtitle track as SRT or VTT. AIFF, CAF and M4R (iPhone ringtone) audio join the audio formats.
