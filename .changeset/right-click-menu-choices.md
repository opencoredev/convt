Choose which formats the "Convert with convt" right-click menu offers for each kind of file: images, SVG, video, audio, PDFs, documents, spreadsheets and presentations. Pick them in the new Settings → Menu tab, in the order you want them, or with `convt menu set audio flac mp3`. Finder, Explorer, Nautilus and `convt targets --menu` follow the choice; for Dolphin, Nemo and Thunar, run `integrations/linux/install.py` again.
