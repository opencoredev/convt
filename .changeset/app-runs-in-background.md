Closing the convt window no longer quits the app. It keeps running with an icon in the menu bar (the system tray on Windows and Linux) so right-click conversions start at once, and leaves the Dock on macOS. Quit from that icon, or with ⌘Q on a Mac and Ctrl+Q elsewhere. "Keep running in the background" in Settings turns this off. The Finder menu shows the colored convt logo. The menu bar icon is a template glyph that follows the menu bar's color, and its menu shows what convt is doing, Convert Files…, your last few converted files (click one to show it in Finder or your file manager) and Check for Updates….
