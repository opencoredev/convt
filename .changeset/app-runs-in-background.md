Closing the convt window no longer quits the app. It keeps running with an icon in the menu bar (the system tray on Windows and Linux) so right-click conversions start at once, and leaves the Dock on macOS. Quit from that icon, or with ⌘Q on a Mac and Ctrl+Q elsewhere. "Keep running in the background" in Settings turns this off on every platform. The menu bar icon is a template glyph that follows the menu bar's color. Its menu shows what convt is doing and offers Convert Files…, your last five converted files (click one to show it in Finder or your file manager), Open convt, Settings…, and Check for Updates… or Restart to Update. The Finder menu shows the colored convt logo.
