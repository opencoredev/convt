Closing the convt window no longer quits the app. It keeps running with an icon in the menu bar (the system tray on Windows and Linux) so right-click conversions start at once, and leaves the Dock on macOS. Quit from that icon, or with ⌘Q on a Mac and Ctrl+Q elsewhere. "Keep running in the background" in Settings turns this off. The Finder menu and the menu bar now show the colored convt logo.
