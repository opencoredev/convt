On Linux desktops that leave window decorations to the app, such as GNOME on Wayland, convt's windows now have a title bar: drag it to move the window, double-click it to maximize, right-click it for the window menu, and use its minimize, maximize and close buttons. Desktops that draw their own title bars keep them.
