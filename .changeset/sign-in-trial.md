The desktop trial now starts when you sign in to convt.app from the app: one 7-day trial per account and per computer. Converting no longer starts a trial, and a trial already running on this computer keeps its remaining days. Signing in also installs a Desktop license you bought, and a Pro subscription in its trial unlocks the app.
