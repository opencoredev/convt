convt now opens MPEG videos (`.mpg`, `.mpeg`), such as DVD rips and older camera and TV recordings, and MP4 files saved with a `.mpg4` extension. Right-click one to turn it into MP4, MOV, WebM, GIF, still images or audio. These formats are read only; convt does not write them.
