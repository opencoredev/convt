FLAC files converted from MP3 (or from a video with MP3 audio) now open in QuickTime and Music instead of failing with "An unknown error occurred (1718449215)". BMP files with transparency now open in Preview and Finder, and keep their transparency. Converting a video that has no sound to an audio format now says the file has no audio track instead of showing an FFmpeg error.
