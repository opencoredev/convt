The desktop app now installs updates itself. While update checks are on, a new version your license covers downloads in the background, is checked against the signed list of releases, and waits for you to click Restart to update. This works for the Mac app, the Windows installer and the Linux AppImage; the deb, rpm and tarball still link to the download page.
